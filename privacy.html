🔮 PoshSight Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

What We Collect

Nothing. PoshSight does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

How It Works

  • All processing happens locally in your browser
  • We don't have servers that receive your data
  • We don't track your browsing activity
  • We don't share any data with third parties

Data Stored Locally

PoshSight stores the following in your browser's local storage (on your device only):

  • Your license key (if Pro user)
  • Daily usage count (resets daily)
  • Cached configuration settings

This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

LemonSqueezy: Used only for payment processing when you purchase Pro. Their privacy policy applies to payment transactions.
